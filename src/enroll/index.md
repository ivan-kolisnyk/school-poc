Enrolling early helps us plan classes and staff for the year.

## Who can enroll

- **Kindergarten to 8th grade, inside our attendance area:** Courtenay is your neighborhood Chicago Public Schools (CPS) school. Check your address with the [CPS School Locator](https://schoolinfo.cps.edu/schoollocator/index.html).
- **Kindergarten to 8th grade, outside our attendance area:** apply through [GoCPS](https://go.cps.edu), the CPS application and lottery website.
- **Preschool:** apply through [Chicago Early Learning](https://www.chicagoearlylearning.org). See [our preschool](/families/preschool/).

{% callout %}
**Kindergarten:** your child must be five years old by September 1.
{% endcallout %}

## How to enroll

1. Get the enrollment forms, in English or Spanish, from the main office.
2. Scan or take photos of the completed forms, your Illinois driver's license or State ID with your current address, and any medical or transfer forms.
3. Email everything to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).
4. The office will call you to set up an appointment to finish enrolling.

## Documents you need

- Completed enrollment forms
- Illinois driver's license or State ID with your current address
- Proof of vaccinations and a physical exam (due by the first day of school)

If they apply to your child:

- Transfer form from your child's last school
- Pre-K placement receipt
- A form from the Illinois State Board of Education (ISBE), if your child is coming from a school outside the district

## Wait list

Open seats depend on how many children from inside our attendance area enroll. If your child is on the wait list, the office will call you in the spring or summer. Please do not call about the wait list.

If your child got a seat through the GoCPS lottery, enroll the same way as above.

## Courtenay preschoolers

Preschoolers who live in our attendance area move up to our kindergarten automatically. Preschoolers who live outside it move to their neighborhood school on June 30.
