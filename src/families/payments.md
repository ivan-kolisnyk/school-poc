To pay a school fee or give a donation:

1. Open [Courtenay's online payment site](https://magic.collectorsolutions.com/magic-ui/Login/cps30141-courtenay-language-art).
2. Choose **One-Time Payment**.
3. Fill out the form and pay.

Questions about a payment? Call the main office at [(773) 534-5790](tel:+17735345790).
