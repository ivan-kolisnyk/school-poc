## About our preschool

The Courtenay Child-Parent Center (CPC) serves children ages 3 to 5. We support the whole family and help children grow from preschool through 3rd grade.

- Two full-day Pre-K classrooms: one for 3-year-olds and one for 4-year-olds.
- We use the Creative Curriculum. Children learn reading and writing, math, science, social studies, art and technology through play at interest centers.
- Teachers and teacher assistants are trained in early childhood education.
- Our parent room and parent resource teacher offer daily activities and workshops for families.

## Who can apply

Your child must be 3 or 4 years old on or before September 1 of the school year. Full-day seats go to 4-year-olds first.

## How to apply

Apply through [Chicago Early Learning](https://www.chicagoearlylearning.org). The application has several steps, and the Chicago Public Schools (CPS) central office assigns the seats. Living near Courtenay does not guarantee a seat.

Once your child has a seat, [enroll the same way as for kindergarten to 8th grade](/enroll/).
