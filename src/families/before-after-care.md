## After-school care

After-school care runs every school day until 5:00 pm. Both programs charge tuition, and both offer help paying it.

### Pre-K (ages 3 to 5): Metro YMCA

[Register with the Metro YMCA](https://community.ymcachicago.org/s/registration).

### Kindergarten to 8th grade: Right at School

[Register with Right at School](https://www.rightatschool.com/school/?school=mary-e-courtenay-language-arts-center&district=il-chicago).

For questions about care before school, call the main office at [(773) 534-5790](tel:+17735345790).

## Classes after school

Academic and enrichment classes run from dismissal to 4:00 pm, one day a week. Each class lasts one quarter. We send sign-up forms home to enrolled students.

## Sports

Students can join CPS sports teams in fall, winter and spring. See [Athletics](/families/athletics/).
