## Local School Council (LSC)

The Local School Council is a group of parents, community members, staff and the principal. It helps make decisions about the school's plan and budget.

For the 2026–27 meeting dates, ask the main office or call [(773) 534-5790](tel:+17735345790).

{% callout %}
Current agendas and minutes are available from the main office.
{% endcallout %}

## Parent Advisory Council (PAC)

The Parent Advisory Council is a group of Courtenay parents who meet during the school year. PAC meeting notices and agendas are available from the main office. We also share them in [News & Events](/news/) when they are posted.
