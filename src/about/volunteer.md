Chicago Public Schools (CPS) asks every volunteer to apply before they start.

1. Read about [CPS volunteer programs](https://www.cps.edu/services-and-supports/parent-engagement/volunteer-programs/).
2. Fill out the [CPS volunteer application](https://chicagopublicschools.civicore.com/).

Questions? Call the main office at [(773) 534-5790](tel:+17735345790).
