## Our vision

Courtenay gives every student a school that challenges their mind and cares for their feelings, at every ability level. Students build strong thinking, social and technology skills. They leave ready to do good work in their community and in the wider world.

## Our mission

- Run strong programs that connect school, home and community.
- Build a school where everyone belongs and where we expect excellence, responsibility and respect for differences.
- Grow students' thinking, social and technology skills through reading and writing, teacher training, and support from parents and the community.

## Our values

- **Respect** for ourselves, others and our community
- **Curiosity** and a love of learning
- **Kindness** in everything we do
