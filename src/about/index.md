Mary E. Courtenay Language Arts Center is a Chicago public school at 4420 N. Beacon St. We work to give every child a safe, welcoming place to learn.

## Our values

- **Respect** for ourselves, others and our community
- **Curiosity** and a love of learning
- **Kindness** in everything we do
