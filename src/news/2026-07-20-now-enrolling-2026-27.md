We are accepting new students in **Pre-K to 8th grade** for the 2026–27 school year. Enrollment is for families who live in our attendance area.

For kindergarten, your child must be five years old by **September 1, 2026**.

## How to enroll

1. Check that your address is in our attendance area with the [CPS School Locator](https://schoolinfo.cps.edu/schoollocator/index.html).
2. Get the enrollment forms from the main office and fill them out.
3. Email the completed forms to [Courtenay_Tigers@cps.edu](mailto:Courtenay_Tigers@cps.edu).
4. The office will call you to set up an appointment to finish enrollment.

Questions? Call the main office at [(773) 534-5790](tel:+17735345790).
