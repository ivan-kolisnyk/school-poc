## Enroll (old: "How to Enroll at Courtenay")
- Neighborhood school for K–8 students living in the attendance boundary. Check: https://schoolinfo.cps.edu/schoollocator/index.html
- Out-of-boundary K–8: through the GoCPS Options for Knowledge lottery (https://go.cps.edu). Preschool: through Chicago Early Learning (https://www.chicagoearlylearning.org).
- Enroll as early as possible (helps staffing).
- Wait list: seats depend on in-boundary enrollment; the school calls wait-listed families in spring/summer — please don't call about the wait list. Lottery-offered families register the same way.
- Preschool: living in the area doesn't guarantee a seat; central office runs a multi-step online application (Chicago Early Learning). Once assigned, enroll like K–8.
- Current Courtenay preschoolers in the attendance area are automatically enrolled in kindergarten; those outside the area transfer to their home school on June 30.
- Kindergarten: child must be five by September 1.
- Steps: 1) complete the enrollment forms (English/Spanish; forms available from the main office — old site had no direct file); 2) scan forms, parent's Illinois driver's license or State ID with current address, and any medical/transfer forms; 3) email everything to Courtenay_Tigers@cps.edu; the office calls to schedule an appointment to finish.
- Documents: completed forms; Illinois driver's license/State ID with current address; proof of vaccinations and physicals (due by the first day of school); if applicable: transfer form from previous school, Pre-K placement receipt, ISBE form from out-of-district school.

## Preschool (Courtenay Child-Parent Center, CPC)
- Ages 3–5; focus on the whole family and continuity from preschool through grade 3.
- Eligibility: child is 3 or 4 on or before September 1 of the school year; 4-year-olds prioritized for full-day.
- Two full-day Pre-K classrooms (ages 3 and 4). Creative Curriculum: literacy, math, science, social studies, arts, technology; interest centers.
- Early-childhood-trained teachers and assistants; parent room and parent resource teacher with daily activities and workshops.
- Tours: (773) 534-5790 ext. 4-2874; Maria Marquez Rodriguez mnmarquezrodriguez@cps.edu; Dara Ramirez dmramirez@cps.edu.
- Apply through Chicago Early Learning.
